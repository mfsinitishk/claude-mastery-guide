# Risk Management

## Identifying and Mitigating Risks in AI Adoption

---

## 🎯 Overview

Proactively identify, assess, and mitigate risks associated with enterprise AI tool adoption.

**Time to Master:** 3-4 hours

---

## ⚠️ Risk Categories

### Technical Risks

**Risk: AI-Generated Code Vulnerabilities**
- Likelihood: Medium
- Impact: High
- Mitigation: Mandatory code review, security scanning, testing

**Risk: Dependency on Third-Party AI**
- Likelihood: High
- Impact: Medium
- Mitigation: Multi-provider strategy, fallback options

**Risk: Data Leakage**
- Likelihood: Low
- Impact: Critical
- Mitigation: Data filtering, audit logs, compliance checks

### Operational Risks

**Risk: Over-Reliance on AI**
- Likelihood: Medium
- Impact: Medium
- Mitigation: Maintain human expertise, critical thinking training

**Risk: Quality Degradation**
- Likelihood: Medium
- Impact: High
- Mitigation: Quality gates, automated testing, peer review

**Risk: Performance Issues at Scale**
- Likelihood: Medium
- Impact: Medium
- Mitigation: Load testing, capacity planning, optimization

### Business Risks

**Risk: ROI Not Achieved**
- Likelihood: Low
- Impact: High
- Mitigation: Clear metrics, pilot validation, iterative rollout

**Risk: Regulatory Non-Compliance**
- Likelihood: Low
- Impact: Critical
- Mitigation: Legal review, compliance framework, audits

**Risk: Intellectual Property Issues**
- Likelihood: Low
- Impact: High
- Mitigation: Usage policies, code attribution, legal guidance

---

## 🛡️ Risk Mitigation Framework

### 1. Identification
- Regular risk assessments
- Team feedback collection
- Incident tracking
- Industry best practices review

### 2. Assessment
- Risk probability matrix
- Impact analysis
- Priority scoring
- Cost-benefit analysis

### 3. Mitigation Planning
- Preventive controls
- Detective controls
- Corrective controls
- Contingency plans

### 4. Monitoring
- Key risk indicators
- Regular audits
- Continuous improvement
- Escalation procedures

---

## 📋 Risk Mitigation Checklist

### Before Rollout
- [ ] Security audit completed
- [ ] Compliance review approved
- [ ] Data handling policies established
- [ ] Incident response plan ready
- [ ] Insurance coverage reviewed

### During Rollout
- [ ] Monitoring dashboards active
- [ ] Support team prepared
- [ ] Escalation paths defined
- [ ] Rollback procedures tested
- [ ] Communication plan ready

### Post-Rollout
- [ ] Metrics tracked continuously
- [ ] Feedback collected regularly
- [ ] Risks reassessed quarterly
- [ ] Policies updated as needed
- [ ] Lessons learned documented

---

## 🚨 Incident Response

**Preparation:**
- Define incident types
- Establish response team
- Create runbooks
- Test procedures

**Detection:**
- Automated monitoring
- User reporting
- Regular audits
- Compliance checks

**Response:**
1. Assess severity
2. Contain impact
3. Investigate root cause
4. Implement fix
5. Verify resolution
6. Document learnings

**Recovery:**
- Restore normal operations
- Update policies
- Retrain as needed
- Communicate outcomes
