# Level 4 Assessment

## Team Scale Skills Evaluation

---

## 🎯 Assessment Overview

Validate team-scale AI adoption capabilities.

**Time Required:** 4-5 hours  
**Passing Score:** 80% or higher

---

## 📊 Competency Areas

1. Team Enablement (15 points)
2. Organizational Adoption (20 points)
3. Standardization & Practices (20 points)
4. SDLC Integration (15 points)
5. Compliance & Governance (15 points)
6. Cross-Team Collaboration (15 points)

**Total:** 100 points

---

## 🏆 Passing Criteria

**80-100 points:** Advanced to Level 5  
**70-79 points:** Review weak areas  
**Below 70:** Practice exercises
