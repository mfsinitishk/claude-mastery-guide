## Listening Strategies

### Time-Efficient Learning
**1.5x Speed:** Most content digestible at faster speed
**Playlists:** Curate by topic or difficulty
**Show Notes:** Review links and resources
**Selective:** Skip irrelevant episodes

### Weekly Listening Schedule

- Mon/Wed/Fri: Technical podcasts
