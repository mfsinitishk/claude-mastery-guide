    Video Quality Guidelines¶

    Rating Criteria¶

    • 5 Stars: Professional production, accurate, comprehensive
    • 4 Stars: Good quality, accurate, minor issues
    • 3 Stars: Acceptable, some inaccuracies or outdated
    • 2 Stars: Poor quality or significantly outdated
    • 1 Star: Inaccurate or misleading

    • ✓ Verified: Content reviewed and accurate (May 2026)

    Contribution Guidelines¶

    To suggest video resources: 1. Verify video is publicly accessible 2. Ensure content is accurate and high-quality 3. Include: title, creator, duration, difficulty, topics 4. Submit via GitHub issue or pull request

    Quality Standards: - Minimum 720p video quality - Clear audio - Accurate technical information - Updated within last 12 months (unless historical)
