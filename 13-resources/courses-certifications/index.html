    Key Selection Criteria¶

    Factor Weight Considerations
    Learning Objectives High Match your goals
    Time Commitment Medium Fit your schedule
    Cost vs Value High ROI potential
    Instructor Quality High Check credentials
    Hands-On Projects High Portfolio building
    Certificate Value Medium Industry recognition

    Decision Framework¶

    1. Define Goals: What role are you targeting?
    2. Assess Level: Current skills vs requirements
    3. Budget: Total investment capacity
    4. Time: Available hours per week
    5. Learning Style: Self-paced vs structured
    6. Outcome: Certification vs skills vs both
