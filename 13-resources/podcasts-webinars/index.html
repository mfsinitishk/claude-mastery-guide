    Quality Ratings¶

    Rating Criteria¶

    • 5 Stars: Exceptional content, production, insights
    • 4 Stars: High quality, valuable, well-produced
    • 3 Stars: Good content, some value
    • 2 Stars: Basic information, low production
    • 1 Star: Poor quality, outdated

    Verification¶

    • ✓ Active: Regular new episodes
    • ⚠️ Irregular: Sporadic updates
    • ✗ Inactive: No recent episodes (6+ months)

    Community Contributions¶

    Starting Your Own Podcast¶

    Equipment Needed: - Microphone ($100-$300) - Recording software (free-$200) - Hosting ($0-$20/month)

    Topics Needed: - Local AI communities - Niche technical topics - Industry-specific applications - Non-English content

    Platforms: - Anchor (free hosting) - Buzzsprout ($12-$24/month) - Transistor ($19-$99/month)

    Pro Tips: 1. Subscribe to 5-7 core podcasts 2. Use speed controls (1.2x-2x) 3. Take notes on key insights 4. Share episodes with team 5. Join podcast communities 6. Attend live recordings when possible
