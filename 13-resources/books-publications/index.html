    Reading Lists by Role¶

    Software Developer Path¶

    Foundation (1-2 months): 1. Building LLM Applications - Alto (450 pages) 2. Prompt Engineering for Developers - Yang & Mehta (380 pages) 3. Selected research papers (10-15)

    Intermediate (2-3 months): 1. LangChain in Action - Wilson (520 pages) 2. Advanced RAG Patterns - Briggs (340 pages) 3. Technical whitepapers (5-8)

    Advanced (3-4 months): 1. Production LLM Systems - Taylor (420 pages) 2. Deep Learning - Goodfellow et al. (800 pages) 3. Recent conference papers (20+)

    Total Investment: 6-9 months, ~3,000 pages

    Product Manager Path¶

    Essential (2-3 months): 1. AI Product Management - Angelova (360 pages) 2. The AI Advantage - Davenport (280 pages) 3. Industry reports (5-8)

    Advanced (2-3 months): 1. Implementing AI at Scale - Ng (350 pages) 2. Designing AI Experiences - Clark (280 pages) 3. Case studies collection (10-15)

    Total Investment: 4-6 months, ~1,800 pages

    Executive/Leadership Path¶

    Strategic Overview (1 month): 1. AI-First Enterprise - Kozyrkov (320 pages) 2. State of AI Report (180 pages) 3. AI Index Report (380 pages)

    Deep Dive (2-3 months): 1. AI Governance - O'Neil (290 pages) 2. McKinsey AI Reports (200+ pages) 3. Industry-specific whitepapers (10+)

    Total Investment: 3-4 months, ~1,500 pages

    Researcher Path¶

    Core Texts (3-4 months): 1. Deep Learning - Goodfellow et al. (800 pages) 2. Speech and Language Processing - Jurafsky & Martin (600 pages) 3. Pattern Recognition and ML - Bishop (750 pages)

    Specialization (4-6 months): 1. Recent NeurIPS proceedings (selected papers) 2. Constitutional AI and related papers 3. Specialized domain papers (30-50)

    Total Investment: 7-10 months, extensive
