# Compliance

## Ensuring AI-Assisted Development Meets Regulatory Requirements

---

## 🎯 Overview

Maintain compliance with industry regulations (SOC2, HIPAA, PCI-DSS) when using AI tools.

**Time to Master:** 4 hours

---

## 🔧 Compliance Areas

**Data Privacy:** No sensitive data to AI  
**Audit Trails:** Log all AI interactions  
**Access Control:** Role-based AI access  
**Regulatory:** Meet industry-specific requirements
